================================ READ THIS =================================

  TLS IS OFF (ingress.tls.enabled=false).

  Operator sessions are cookies. In plaintext they are sniffable and
  REPLAYABLE, so authentication here stops casual and unauthorised access
  but does NOT stop an on-path attacker.

  This is only appropriate for a localhost evaluation. Enable TLS before
  this reaches any network you do not control.

============================================================================

The dashboard is also the reverse proxy for /webhooks, /api, /gw and /wk, which
is what puts all four services on one origin. Two things follow:

  * It must stay at the origin ROOT. Every redirect target in the services is
    "/" and the session cookie paths are absolute, so there is no sub-path
    deployment.
  * No service port is exposed, which is what makes trustedProxies meaningful:
    nothing can bypass the proxy to forge a forwarded header.

Check it:

  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/name=spire
  kubectl port-forward svc/spire-ui 34700:8080
  ./deploy/e2e.sh http://localhost:34700 <your-idp-url>
